WHO WE ARE

Undertaking many Lightning Protection and Earthing Projects in India

Techno Power is a reliable Importer of ESE Lightning Arrester, manufacturer & service provider of Techno Earthing Electrode, GI Earthing Wire, GI Earthing Plate, Chemical Earthing Electrodes, Techno Copper Bonded Rod, Pipe In Pipe Electrode, GI Earthing Electrode, Ground Enrichment Material, Earthing Pit Covers, and many products.

The range is designed as per current market trends and customer specifications. The products are in compliance with national and international quality standards. We use high-grade raw material and advanced technology in the fabrication of the gamut.

We also specialize in providing Pit covers to conceal the electrodes from external physical damage and thus provide preventive maintenance to the system. We are equipped to provide consultation services and Earthing solutions to a variety of businesses and industry.
